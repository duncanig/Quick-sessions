Rocket Refuel

A moon base mission for the whole room. Mission Control shows a clue and a word with letters missing. The fuel console, on the next wall, is where the team types the missing letters. Each correct letter fills the rocket's tank on the launch pad, and the last wall logs every word as it is completed. Fill every word and the rocket launches. To make your own, change the words and clues in Edit, or build it on a laptop and share it as a link.

Concept prototype for customer research. Laid out for a cube-shaped room with a corner every 2,000 px: all text stays 150 px clear of each corner, and only the moonscape, pipes, lights and effects cross them. Turn on Corner guides to check. The moon base is an illustration drawn in the browser. You can type letters on a keyboard or press them on the console wall. The sample words and clues are written for illustration, so check them before using them with students. The recorded voice, sound effects and music are built into this page. Voice clips only exist for the sample clues: any clue you edit is read by the browser's own voice instead, which may not work on every device. Sound needs a click first, because browsers block audio until then. Edits last until the page is closed unless you share a link, and share links work on the hosted copy of this page. This has not been tested on a real display yet.
