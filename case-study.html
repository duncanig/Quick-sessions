Case Study Room

A branching case for the whole room to work through. The group discusses each decision, takes a quick show of hands, and the lecturer picks the option. The story changes with every choice. To use a different case, change the scenes in Edit or paste one written by an AI tool.

Concept prototype for customer research. The sample case is fictional and written for illustration, so a lecturer should check any legal or professional detail before teaching from it. Edits last until the page is closed. This has not been tested on a real display yet.
