Escape Room

A team works through locked doors against the clock. Each door is one puzzle: a code lock, a multiple-choice question, a sequence to put in order, or a set of pairs to match. The sample is a nursing and healthcare case. To make your own, change the story and puzzles in Edit, or build it on a laptop and share it as a link.

Concept prototype for customer research. The sample clinical content is fictional and written for teaching illustration, so a lecturer should check every clinical detail before using it with students. Edits last until the page is closed unless you share a link. Share links work on the hosted copy of this page. This has not been tested on a real display yet.
