Escape Room

The team stands inside an illustrated ward and looks for the glowing object on the walls. Clicking it reveals the lock: a code, a multiple-choice question, a sequence to put in order, or pairs to match. The sample is a nursing and healthcare case. To make your own, change the story and puzzles in Edit, move the hotspots, or build it on a laptop and share it as a link.

Concept prototype for customer research. The ward is an illustration drawn in the browser, not a photograph. To use real photos or renders instead, paste the web address of an 8000 × 1080 picture into Edit, then place the hotspots on it. Laid out for a cube-shaped room with a corner every 2,000 px: all text stays 150 px clear of each corner, and only the picture and light effects cross them. Turn on Corner guides to check. Sound needs a click first, because browsers block audio until then. The sample room's questions use recorded voice clips built into this page, so they work without any speech support. Any line you edit has no recording and falls back to the browser's built-in voice, which depends on the device; the music is generated in the browser. The sample clinical content is fictional and written for teaching illustration, so a lecturer should check every clinical detail before using it with students. Edits last until the page is closed unless you share a link, and share links work on the hosted copy of this page. This has not been tested on a real display yet.
