Panorama Sessions

Ready-made sessions drawn for a very wide display. The teacher changes the text in Edit, then presses Play. On a laptop the stage is scaled to fit, and on the real display it runs at full size.

Concept prototype for customer research. Edits last until the page is closed. Key content sits in the middle band of the stage, away from the top and bottom edges, so it should survive some edge distortion. This has not been tested on a real display yet.
